The sloth promise.

Sloth Mode has two separate connections. You choose when to use them.

⌂
Local pairing

Your browser and home computer communicate directly. Your notes never cross the internet.

↗
Account-linked devices

Cloudflare stores a device name, token hash and last-seen timestamp. Not your tasks or prompts.

☁

Not yet enabled. A later opt-in feature will connect to your PC securely while away.
