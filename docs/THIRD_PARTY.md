# Third-party software and licenses

No model weights are shipped. Gemma models retain their model-specific license and distribution terms; users approve separate downloads. `llama.cpp` is developed by its own maintainers and retains its separate license, even if installed alongside Sloth Mode. The release does not claim these are owned or licensed as Sloth Mode source code.

Sloth Mode integrates with external services such as Cloudflare, GitHub, Google, SerpApi and ElevenLabs; those services' marks and APIs are subject to their own terms.

The project's optional promotional video was created with a workflow inspired by [`latent-spaces/brag`](https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag), which is MIT licensed. The video is not bundled in the source repository; verify music and media licenses before reusing assets from external sources.
